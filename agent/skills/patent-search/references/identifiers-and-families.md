# Patent identifiers and family checks

- Preserve the publication authority/country code, number and kind code as shown by the source (for example, `WO...A1` or `US...A1`). Do not silently drop the kind code.
- Keep publication number, application number, grant number and priority number in separate fields. Keep filing, priority, publication and grant dates distinct.
- A shared title, applicant or similar abstract does not establish a patent family. Use a displayed family identifier or verify the priority relationship from the publication record.
- A family groups related publications; it does not make country-specific documents, claims, legal status or dates interchangeable. Keep the family members visible when they matter to the user's question.
- Treat third-party status labels as leads. Legal-status claims need a dated check against the relevant official register; otherwise report status as unverified.
- Search result snippets and OCR text are not sufficient evidence for exact identifiers, claims or legal status. Check the source page or official document.
